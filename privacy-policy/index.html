ClickTo3D Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 31, 2026

ClickTo3D respects the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy explains how the ClickTo3D Autodesk Revit add-in handles information, and how this website handles the optional email sign-up.

1. Information Collected and How It Is Used

ClickTo3D does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information.

ClickTo3D does not require users to create an account, sign in, or provide personal information.

ClickTo3D operates locally within Autodesk Revit and does not upload or transmit Revit models, project files, model geometry, drawings, or other project information to ClickTo3D or to external servers.

ClickTo3D may store application preferences locally on the user’s computer, including:

  • 3D box square size
  • Height above the active plan level
  • Height below the active plan level
  • Preferred units
  • 3D view name

These settings are used only to remember the user’s preferences between Revit sessions. They remain on the user’s computer and are not transmitted to ClickTo3D.

2. Third-Party Sharing

ClickTo3D does not share user data with third parties because ClickTo3D does not collect or transmit personal information or Revit project data.

The ClickTo3D add-in does not use advertising networks, analytics services, tracking technologies, or third-party data collection SDKs.

Autodesk products and services are governed separately by Autodesk’s own terms and privacy policies.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

ClickTo3D does not retain personal information or Revit project data on ClickTo3D servers or systems.

Application preferences stored locally on the user’s computer remain under the user’s control and can be removed from the user’s computer at any time.

Because ClickTo3D does not collect or retain user data remotely, there is no personal or project data held by ClickTo3D that requires server-side deletion.

4. Consent and Requests for Data Deletion

ClickTo3D does not require consent for the collection of personal information because no personal information is collected or transmitted by the add-in.

Since ClickTo3D does not maintain personal information or project data about its users, there is normally no publisher-held user data to delete.

Users with questions about privacy, data handling, or deletion may contact: clickto3d@gmail.com

5. This Website and Email Updates

If you choose to sign up for updates on this website, we collect the email address you enter.

You can unsubscribe at any time by writing to clickto3d@gmail.com.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If a future version of ClickTo3D introduces functionality that changes how information is collected, stored, transmitted, or shared, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
