ClickToSection Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

ClickToSection respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the ClickToSection add-in for Autodesk Revit handles information.

1. Information Collected by ClickToSection

ClickToSection does not collect personal data through the add-in.

ClickToSection does not collect or transmit:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Autodesk account information
  • Revit project or model data
  • File names or project names
  • User activity or usage analytics
  • Device identifiers
  • Location information
  • Advertising information
  • Telemetry or diagnostic data

ClickToSection does not require a ClickToSection account or login.

While the on-screen direction marker is shown, ClickToSection checks the mouse position and whether a key or mouse button is pressed, only to place the section or to cancel. Pressing a key only cancels the aim (Shift, Ctrl, Alt, the Windows keys and the lock keys are ignored); which key was pressed is not recorded or stored.

2. Local Application Settings

ClickToSection may save application preferences locally on the user's computer, in the user's Windows AppData folder, including settings such as:

  • Section width, depth, height, and how far it reaches below the level
  • Preferred units
  • Whether the section height follows the level above, and whether changed sizes still have to be applied to the existing section view
  • The section view name
  • Aiming options, such as snapping and the pull distance ring
  • The look of the direction marker, such as its size, line thickness, and color
  • A locally generated code used to keep section view names unique between different users on a shared project

These settings are used only to remember the user's ClickToSection preferences.

They remain on the user's computer and are not transmitted to ClickToSection or any external server.

3. Local Error Logs

If ClickToSection cannot load, or if its on-screen direction marker cannot start, the add-in may write a short technical error log to the Windows temporary folder on the user's computer.

These logs contain technical error details only. Like any Windows file path, a logged path may include the name of the user's Windows folder.

The logs remain on the user's computer, are not transmitted to ClickToSection or any external server, and may be deleted by the user at any time.

4. Revit Project Data

ClickToSection operates inside Autodesk Revit and may create, modify, activate, or reuse Revit views and related Revit elements as part of its normal functionality.

ClickToSection does not transmit Revit models or project information to ClickToSection or any external service.

All model operations performed by the add-in occur locally within the user's Revit environment.

5. Internet and Network Access

ClickToSection does not require an internet connection for its normal functionality.

The ClickToSection add-in does not communicate with ClickToSection servers or other external services and does not transmit user or project information over the internet.

6. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

The ClickToSection Revit add-in does not use:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising networks
  • Tracking technologies
  • Cookies
  • Usage telemetry

7. Third Parties

ClickToSection does not provide user information or Revit project information to third parties because the add-in does not collect or transmit such information.

ClickToSection operates as an add-in for Autodesk Revit and may be distributed through Autodesk Design and Make Marketplace. Autodesk products and services operate independently and are governed by Autodesk's own privacy policies and terms.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because ClickToSection does not collect or receive personal data through the add-in, ClickToSection does not maintain a database of user information or Revit project information.

Locally stored ClickToSection preferences and error logs remain under the user's control on the user's computer and may be reset or removed locally.

ClickToSection does not retain copies of those settings or logs on any ClickToSection server.

9. Consent and Privacy Requests

Because ClickToSection does not collect or transmit personal information through the add-in, no consent for collection of application data is required.

There is therefore no remotely stored ClickToSection application data for a user to revoke consent for or request deletion of.

Users may contact ClickToSection with any privacy-related questions or requests using the contact information below.

10. Support Communications

If a user voluntarily contacts ClickToSection by email for technical support, the information the user chooses to provide, such as an email address, message, screenshots, or attachments, may be used solely for purposes such as responding to the inquiry, troubleshooting the reported problem, and providing support.

ClickToSection uses Gmail, a service provided by Google, to receive and respond to support emails. Information voluntarily sent by email may therefore be processed by Google as necessary to provide the email service.

Users should avoid sending confidential Revit project information unless it is necessary for their support request.

Support communications are not sold or used for advertising.

A user may request deletion of personal information contained in support correspondence by contacting ClickToSection at the email address below, subject to any information that must reasonably be retained for legal, security, or record-keeping purposes.

11. Security

Because the ClickToSection add-in does not transmit or centrally store user or project data, such information is not maintained on ClickToSection servers.

Users remain responsible for the security of their own computer, Autodesk Revit environment, and Revit project files.

12. Children's Privacy

ClickToSection is a professional productivity add-in designed for users of Autodesk Revit and is not directed specifically toward children.

ClickToSection does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the add-in.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if ClickToSection's functionality or privacy practices change.

Any updated policy will include a revised effective date.

If a future version of ClickToSection introduces functionality that collects or transmits information, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
