The ClickTo3D add-in

ClickTo3D runs inside Autodesk Revit on your computer. It does not collect, send or store any personal data or model data. Your settings (box size, units and 3D view name) are saved only on your own computer.

This website

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Email updates

If you sign up for updates, we keep your email address only to tell you when a new app is released. We never sell or share it. Every email includes a way to unsubscribe, or you can ask us to delete your address at any time.

Sign-up addresses are stored in a private Google Sheet that only we can access.

Downloads

ClickTo3D is distributed through the Autodesk App Store. Autodesk’s own privacy policy applies to your Autodesk account and downloads.
